# Sample Education Entries

Here are some examples of how to format education entries for the resume creation tool:

## Basic Format
```
B.S. Computer Science
University of California, Berkeley
Graduated: May 2020
GPA: 3.8/4.0
```

## With Additional Details
```
Master of Business Administration (MBA)
Stanford Graduate School of Business
Completed: December 2022
Specialization: Technology Management
GPA: 3.9/4.0
Thesis: "Digital Transformation Strategies for Fortune 500 Companies"
```

## Tips for Best Results:
1. Include graduation dates or expected graduation dates
2. Mention GPA if it's 3.5 or higher
3. Add relevant coursework if it aligns with the job
4. Include honors, awards, or academic achievements
5. Mention study abroad or exchange programs if applicable
6. List relevant certifications alongside formal education
